## Other federal findings

Public guidance without a portable implementation: Indian Affairs, USDA department-wide web standards, USDOT, EPA One EPA Web, NCBI, and HHS content guidance. Official evidence but no public implementation: DHS department-wide theme, SSA UX Framework 3.0, Treasury TDDS, and Library of Congress global design system. Legacy-only systems include NASA WDS, NIAID, FCC Design Standards, old CMS child repositories, VA Formation, CFPB Capital Framework, CBP Theme, U.S. Forms System, USAJOBS, and USPTO UI Design Library.
